Use the marketplace responsibly

ARDTIG STORES is a marketplace for browsing game accounts and related digital products. Provide accurate account and payment details, and use the service only where transfer of the offered digital goods is permitted by the relevant game publisher.

Payments

Confirm the order details and amount in Flutterwave checkout before paying. For bank transfers, send the exact amount to the temporary account shown before it expires. An order is processed only after the server confirms payment with Flutterwave. Diamond orders are submitted for admin fulfillment review after payment is confirmed.

Do not send money to anyone claiming to represent the store outside the checkout flow. Contact the store if a payment succeeds but your order status does not update.

Digital goods and refunds

Digital goods may be difficult or impossible to recover after delivery. Once a digital item or account has been delivered, refunds are not offered for an incorrect recipient, incorrect UID, changed mind, or payment made in the wrong amount. Contact the store before paying if any detail is unclear. This does not remove rights that cannot legally be excluded.

Scams and account safety

Never share your password, verification code, or payment credentials in chat. ARDTIG STORES will not ask for your Google password or a one-time sign-in code. Report suspicious messages and use only the official store website.

Marketplace availability

Listings, prices, and availability may change. A listing is delivered only after successful payment confirmation and availability checks. If payment succeeds but a listing cannot be fulfilled, contact the store for assistance.

Contact

For help with an order, contact the store through the account associated with your purchase.
